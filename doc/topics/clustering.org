#+title: Community Detection

*Modules:* [[file:../../graphs/clustering.janet][clustering]]

Modularity-based clustering of directed graphs.

The blocks of a “two cliques joined by one edge” graph are recovered as distinct communities. Modularity-based methods do not resolve overlapping communities. For dense or weighted networks normalise weights first.

* Summary

- =louvain= — multi-level Louvain where every accepted move strictly raises modularity.
- =leiden= — Louvain plus one refinement pass that repairs nodes locked into a suboptimal community during aggregation.
- =community-improve= (an individual local-moving pass) and =aggregate= (the super-node collapse) are building blocks that are not exported from the =graphs= umbrella but are available via =(import graphs/clustering :prefix "")=.

Input digraphs must be symmetric (or undirected edges supplied in both directions)

* Examples

The classic “two triangles joined by a single edge” instance. Note that each undirected edge is written twice (once per direction) to satisfy the symmetry requirement

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results none
(import graphs :prefix "")
#+end_src

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results output
(def g (make-digraph))
(add-nodes g "a" "b" "c" "d" "e" "f")
(add-edges g [["a" "b"] ["b" "a"]
              ["a" "c"] ["c" "a"]
              ["b" "c"] ["c" "b"]
              ["d" "e"] ["e" "d"]
              ["d" "f"] ["f" "d"]
              ["c" "d"] ["d" "c"]])

(def community (louvain g))

(printf "Louvain communities: %s"
        (string/join
         (map |(string/join (sort $) ",")
              (values (group-by (fn [n] (get community n))
                                (keys community)))) " | "))
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
: Louvain communities: a,b,c | d,e,f

Leiden refinement on the same graph which may split or reassign communities from Louvain.

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results output
(def g2 (make-digraph))
(add-nodes g2 ["a" "b" "c" "d" "e" "f"])
(add-edges g2 [["a" "b"] ["b" "a"]
               ["a" "c"] ["c" "a"]
               ["b" "c"] ["c" "b"]
               ["d" "e"] ["e" "d"]
               ["d" "f"] ["f" "d"]
               ["c" "d"] ["d" "c"]])

(def community2 (leiden g2))

(printf "Leiden communities: %s"
        (string/join
         (map |(string/join (sort $) ",")
              (values (group-by (fn [n] (get community2 n))
                                  (keys community2)))) " | "))
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
: Leiden communities: a,b,c | d,e,f
