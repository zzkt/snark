Vertex and edge coloring, and the Turán extremal family.

* Summary

- =greedy-colour= — Welsh–Powell greedy vertex coloring (constructive upper bound of $\Delta(g)+1$)
- =proper-colouring?=, =chromatic-number= — verification and exact $χ$ (branch & bound, exponential in the worst case)
- =vertex-colouring= — a proper vertex coloring with /at most/ =k= colours, or =nil= when there is none. The same branch & bound as =chromatic-number=, stopping at =k= instead of searching for the minimum.
- =edge-colour-greedy=, =vizing-edge-colour=, =edge-chromatic-number= (aka =chromatic-index=) — edge coloring and $χ′$.
- =make-turan-graph=, =turan-number= — the complete r-partite graph $T(n,r)$ on n vertices, and $ex(n, K_{r+1})$ (edge-maximal $K_{r+1}$-free).

* Example

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results none
(import graphs :prefix "")
#+end_src

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results output
(def c4 (make-graph))
(add-nodes c4 ["a" "b" "c" "d"])
(add-edges c4 [["a" "b"] ["b" "c"] ["c" "d"] ["d" "a"]])

(def col (greedy-colour c4))
(printf "χ(C4) = %p  proper? %p" (chromatic-number c4) (proper-colouring? c4 col))

# ex(7, K4): a K_4-free graph on 7 vertices has at most 16 edges.
(printf "ex(7, K4) = %p" (turan-number 7 3))
(printf "2-partite Turán is bipartite: %p" (= 2 (chromatic-number (make-turan-graph 7 2))))
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
: χ(C4) = 2  proper? true
: ex(7, K4) = 16
: 2-partite Turán is bipartite: true
