*Modules:* [[file:../../graphs/random-graph.janet][random-graph]]

Erdős–Rényi sampling and preferential attachment growth.

* Summary

All three build an undirected =:graph= with nodes $0…n-1$ and take an optional trailing =seed= for reproducible output.

- =random-graph= — $G(n,p)$: each of the $n(n-1)/2$ possible edges appears independently with probability $p$.
- =random-graph-edges= — $G(n,m)$: $n$ vertices with exactly $m$ distinct edges, sampled as $m$ distinct slots by Floyd's subset sampling (no rejected draws).
- =barabasi-albert= — scale-free growth. Begin with a clique of $m_0$ seed vertices, then add each new vertex with $m$ edges attached to existing vertices chosen with probability proportional to degree.

$p=0.0$ yields the empty graph, $p=1.0$ the complete graph. Out-of-range edge counts are rejected rather than silently clamped. =random-graph-edges= raises an error when $m > n(n-1)/2$, and =barabasi-albert= raises an error unless $m \leq m_0 < n$. $G(n,p)$ is the binomial random graph, used for exact thresholds and phase transitions.

* Example

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results none
(import graphs :prefix "")
#+end_src

#+begin_src janet :session examples :results output
(def g (random-graph 8 0.5 42))
(printf "G(8, 1/2) edges: %p (density %.2f)" (edge-count g) (density g))

(def gm (random-graph-edges 8 10 7))
(printf "G(8, 10 edges) actual: %p" (edge-count gm))

(def ba (barabasi-albert 20 3 2 7))
(printf "BA(20) max degree: %p"
        (reduce (fn [mx u] (max mx (degree ba u))) 0 (nodes ba)))
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
: G(8, 1/2) edges: 16 (density 0.57)
: G(8, 10 edges) actual: 10
: BA(20) max degree: 8
