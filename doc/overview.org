* Core Data Structures

Concrete graph types; =:graph=, =:digraph=, =:multigraph=, =:multidigraph=, =:weighted-graph=, =:tree= and =:weighted-tree=, which provide a uniform API (e.g. =add-node=, =add-edge=, =nodes=, =neighbours=, =degree=, =edge-count=, etc)

Details: [[file:topics/core.org][core]]

* Traversal & ordering

BFS/DFS, topological sort (Kahn), Kosaraju SCC, =semi-connected?=, Eulerian circuits.

Details: [[file:topics/traversal.org][traversal]]

* Cycle analysis

Acyclicity, undirected cycle-space basis, simple-cycle enumeration.

Details: [[file:topics/cycles.org][cycles]]

* DAG analysis & causal inference

Acyclicity & periodicity, topological generations, ancestors/descendants, transitive closure and reduction, longest paths, antichains, and causal primitives (colliders, v-structures).

Details: [[file:topics/dag.org][dag]]

* Tournaments

Recognition, score sequences (Landau), kings, and the transitive/regular/strong constructions.

Details: [[file:topics/tournaments.org][tournaments]]

* Flow & cut

max-flow (Edmonds–Karp, Dinic), min s–t cut, and expansion-family cut statistics; conductance, edge/node expansion, and volume.

Details: [[file:topics/flow-cut.org][flow-cut]]

* Centrality & ranking

Degree, betweenness (Brandes), closeness, harmonic, eigenvector, Katz and PageRank.

Details: [[file:topics/centrality.org][centrality]]

* Community detection

Multi-level Louvain and Leiden refinement for community detection.

Details: [[file:topics/clustering.org][clustering]]

* Connectivity

Weak/strong components, condensation, bridges, articulation points, biconnected components, vertex/edge connectivity, and offline Tarjan LCA.

Details: [[file:topics/connectivity.org][connectivity]]

* Planarity

Planarity tests for undirected graphs, Demoucron Malgrange Pertuiset (DMP)

Details: [[file:topics/planarity.org][planarity]]

* Petri nets

Place/transition nets with weighted arcs, firing semantics with capacities, reachability and state spaces, Karp–Miller coverability, liveness, and exact invariants.

Details: [[file:topics/petri-net.org][petri-net]]

* Metrics

Density, average degree, diameter/radius/center, average path length, transitivity, and  clustering.

Details: [[file:topics/metrics.org][metrics]]

* Similarity

Jaccard and cosine neighbourhood similarity, brute-force k-NN.

Details: [[file:topics/similarity.org][similarity]]

* Cores & shells

Core numbers by minimum-degree peeling, onion ordering, and the k-shell, k-crust, k-corona layers.

Details: [[file:topics/cores.org][cores]]

* Graph colouring

Greedy colouring (Welsh–Powell) and chromatic number, edge colouring with Vizing, and the Turán extremal constructions.

Details: [[file:topics/colouring.org][colouring]]

* Matching, cliques & coverings

Edmonds' blossom for general graphs, bipartite matchings, König vertex-cover/independent-set duality, Hall's condition, and clique enumeration.

Details: [[file:topics/matching.org][matching]]

* Spanning trees

Spanning-tree counts and BEST Eulerian circuit counts via the Laplacian.

Details: [[file:topics/spanning-tree.org][spanning-tree]]

* Random graphs

G(n,p) and G(n,m) Erdős–Rényi sampling plus Barabási–Albert preferential attachment.

Details: [[file:topics/random-graphs.org][random-graphs]]

* Degree sequences

Graphicality tests, Erdős–Gallai, Havel–Hakimi, and the multi/pseudo/digraphical relaxations.

Details: [[file:topics/sequence.org][sequence]]

* Shortest paths & weighted graphs

Dijkstra shortest paths, Prim MST, all-pairs distance statistics, Floyd–Warshall.

Details: [[file:topics/shortest-paths.org][shortest-paths]]

* Suffix trees & strings

Suffix trees and compressed suffix trees (CST) for exact pattern occurrence queries and suffix-array navigation.

Details: [[file:topics/strings.org][strings]]

* Hamiltonian paths

Hamilton(ian) cycle and path search with Dirac's sufficient condition and deterministic backtracking.

Details: [[file:topics/hamiltonian.org][hamiltonian]]

* Other algorithms

Binary min-heap and offline LCA.

Details: [[file:topics/other.org][other]]

* Graph import

Import graphs from adjacency lists/matrices, DOT and GEXF formats, and House of Graphs.

Details: [[file:topics/import.org][import]]

* Graph export

Deterministic (mostly) DOT, GEXF, SVG, TikZ (LaTeX), and self-contained cytoscape.js HTML.

Details: [[file:topics/export.org][export]]

* Graph layout

Hierarchical layering; force-directed and spring placement; circle, tree, radial, orthogonal, spectral layouts; Lombardi style circular-arc and poly-arc drawings; and various rectilinear phylograms.

Details: [[file:topics/layout.org][layout]]
